# Privacy Policy — Astrande

**Effective date: September 18, 2026**

## About Astrande

Astrande is an Android video game developed and published by Skylante.

## Data Collection

Astrande does not collect or transmit personal information.

The game does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or online services.

Game save data is stored locally on the user's device.

## Data Sharing

Astrande does not share personal information with Skylante or any third party.

## Storage and Security

Local game save data is stored on the user's device and protected using encryption. Skylante does not have access to these local save files.

## Children's Privacy

Astrande does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Astrande's data practices change.
